C1

Credit score exactly on the tier boundary

Correct

Expected — verified against the policy chunk files

600 = Tier 2 (meets the minimum, no mandatory deposit). 599 = Tier 3 (550+), which carries a mandatory 20% deposit on EVERY application, not conditional on contract type.

Question

A BFS commercial applicant has an Experian CCR score of exactly 600. Another has 599. What changes between them?

At 600, they qualify for BFS PRIME Tier 2.

At 599, they don’t reach Tier 2, but they still meet PRIME Tier 3 because Tier 3 starts at 550.

So the key change is: 600 = Tier 2, 599 = Tier 3.

Practical differences:

  • Tier 2 gets full PRIME access and no automatic tier deposit.
  • Tier 3 still sits in PRIME, but needs a 20% deposit on every application.
  • Tier 3 can still do commercial used vehicles; the used commercial restriction only kicks in at Tier 4.

11 sources · 5.3s

Assessment Treated 600 as the qualifying minimum. Also caught that the used-vehicle limit is Tier 4, not Tier 3.

C7

A product tier that does not exist

Correct

Expected — verified against the policy chunk files

CFAL has NO Low Doc option -- reject the premise. Documentation scales with transaction size; financial statements must be no more than 18 months old, for all related companies and trusts.

Question

What are CFAL's Low Doc requirements for a $150,000 transaction?

CFAL doesn’t have a Low Doc option.

For a $150,000 standard transaction, you’d be in the ≤$250k document band. CFAL would need the usual full-doc pack, including:

  • Client info
  • Brief background on the business/directors
  • ABN for 2+ years and GST registered
  • Full asset description and reason for purchase
  • Signed privacy forms for all individuals
  • Last 2 years financial statements and tax returns
  • Current A&L for all directors/guarantors
  • Commitment schedule

If it fits DriveXpress fast-track criteria, financials may not be needed — but that’s not “Low Doc” as such; it’s a separate fast-track pathway.
