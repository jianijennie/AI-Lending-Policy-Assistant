Fail — 9 questions where the answer would mislead a broker

Sorted by lender. Click a row to see the question, reference answer, and what the model actually said.

RES-01FailEligibilityCites a 2-year "Standard tier" threshold instead of the real 1-year Basic-tier rule — right conclusion, wrong figure.
Question

I registered my ABN 8 months ago and I'm about to buy a delivery van for my new business. Can I get finance through Resimac, and if not, what's missing?

Reference

Resimac's lowest tier, Basic, still requires the ABN and GST registration to both be more than 1 year old. At 8 months you're below every tier.

Model said

"…Resimac requires at least 2 years of ABN registration for the Standard tier, which is the minimum tier you can qualify for."

Root cause: resimac_customer_tiers chunk wording likely blurs the Basic (1yr) vs Standard (2yr) tier boundary. Fix: clarify which tier is actually the floor.
RES-04FailLoan limitsNon-deterministic regression — reverted to conflating the aggregate exposure limit with the single-deal NAF cap after passing a manual retest.
Question

I run an established company and want to finance $500,000 worth of new equipment in one deal. Can Resimac fund that?

Reference

Not at the standard limit — Full Doc max NAF under the top tiers is $450,000, $50k short. Split the purchase, use a lender with a higher single-transaction ceiling, or ask Resimac for an exception.

Model said

"…the maximum exposure limit for an SME is capped at $500,000 across all contracts, which means you couldn't borrow that amount in one go…"

Root cause: this exact question was confirmed fixed right after a prompt edit distinguishing exposure limits from NAF caps — but a fresh sample reverted to the old confusion. The prompt instruction isn't reliable across generations. Fix: separate the NAF and exposure figures more structurally in resimac_loan_limits rather than relying on prompt wording alone.
BFS-04FailLoan limitsDirectly contradicts the reference — wrongly declines an application the policy actually approves.
Question

I have an Experian CCR of 850 and want to finance a $350,000 vehicle. Can BFS approve this?

Reference

Yes — 850 places you in Tier 1 (CCR ≥800), which qualifies for High Value lending up to $400,000. $350,000 fits comfortably.

Model said

"…since your CCR doesn't meet the Ultra Prime criteria (which requires a score of 960), BFS won't be able to approve the financing for that amount."

Root cause: model appears to misread which tiers unlock the High Value product, inventing a 960-score gate. Fix: check bfs_vehicle_types / bfs_customer_tiers for ambiguity around High Value eligibility.
BFS-05FailEligibilityNever mentions the visa-holder income thresholds that actually govern this question.
Question

I'm on a temporary skilled work visa earning $85,000 a year and want to finance a car. Am I eligible for BFS PRIME or would I need the Plus product?

Reference

PRIME needs $100k p.a. for visa holders — you don't qualify. Plus has a $50k bar, so you'd be assessed under Plus.

Model said

"…To confirm your options, could you let me know your Experian CCR score?" (no mention of the income thresholds at all)

Root cause: the visa income-threshold chunk likely isn't ranking highly enough for this phrasing. Fix: check retrieval for visa-related eligibility questions; consider trigger-word additions.
CFAL-03FailSpecial programsLender mis-detection — the word "Westpac" in the question pulls in the wrong lender's chunks entirely.
Question

I've had a CFAL/Westpac equipment loan running for 14 months with a clean payment record, and I want to roll it into a new facility. Am I eligible for Rollover?

Reference

Yes — CFAL's Rollover needs 12+ months, 14 clears it. $500k cap rolling into a CFAL/Westpac-originated facility, $250k into another financier's.

Model said

Retrieved sources: westpac_rollover, westpac_replacement, westpac_exclusions… (zero CFAL chunks). "If you're an existing client with Westpac, you should be eligible to roll over your contract…"

Root cause: detect_lender() picks "Westpac" over "CFAL" because the question names both — and CFAL is itself a Westpac Group brand, so this collision is structural, not incidental. Fix: CFAL should win when both keywords appear together.
ANG-05FailDocumentationDrops two of the explicitly required documents at the $500k–$1m band.
Question

I want to borrow $700,000 for equipment through Angle. What financial documentation will I need to provide?

Reference

FY2024+FY2023 financials only (no bank-statement alternative at this size), plus an aged debtor/creditor listing and cash flow projections, plus business background.

Model said

Listed FY24/23 financials, Commitment Schedule, ATO portal statements, business background — no mention of the aged debtor/creditor listing or cash flow projections.

Root cause: retrieval/synthesis drops two explicitly-required document types for this size band. Fix: verify angle_doc_types chunk lists all $500k–$1m items clearly and check they're all surfacing.
ANG-07FailLoan limitsFabricates a wrong cap and denies a real $400k program even though the correct chunk was in context.
Question

My business has been going for 3 years with a 620 credit score, and I want to borrow $380,000 with minimal paperwork. Is there a low-doc option that stretches that high? (no lender named)

Reference

Yes — Angle's dedicated $400k Low Doc program (3+ yr ABN/GST, 600+ score) covers exactly this.

Model said

"…there isn't a specific Low Doc program that extends to $380,000. The maximum amount for Low Doc loans is generally capped at $150,000…"

Root cause: question names no lender, so the system fans out across all 7 lenders; angle_400k_low_doc is retrieved but lost in the noise of the wider pool. Fix: investigate whether fan-out context is too large for reliable single-chunk synthesis on numeric eligibility questions.
FLX-02FailPricingCites the wrong age rule for the exact condition the question hinges on, and omits the rate.
Question

My Road Transport & Logistics business runs 6 trucks and has been established for years. Can I access flexicommercial's flexipremium pricing on a used prime mover that's 4 years old?

Reference

Yes — the fleet exception allows used trucks up to 5 years old (yours at 4 qualifies). Base rate ~7.30% + the standard 1.0% prime mover add-on.

Model said

"…the prime mover needs to be within the asset age limit of up to 20 years at the end of the term…" (no rate given)

Root cause: conflates the general 15–20yr end-of-term loading band with the specific 5-year fleet-exception age cap — the actual crux of the question. Fix: make the 5-year limit more clearly distinguished in flexi_flexipremium.
MET-07FailPricingReports only one of two loadings that both apply and stack.
Question

I want to finance a used passenger vehicle that's 11 years old at the start of the loan and will be 13 years old at the end of the term. How does Metro's age loading work here?

Reference

Two loadings stack: +0.75% (over 5yrs at start) and +1.50% (over 10yrs at end).

Model said

"…an additional loading of 1.50% will apply." (the +0.75% start-of-term loading is never mentioned)

Root cause: only the end-of-term loading is surfaced; the two loadings aren't flagged as additive. Fix: check metro_interest_rates / prompt for whether stacking loadings are clearly marked as cumulative.
